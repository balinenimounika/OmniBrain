Key Modules: 
• 
Multi-Agent Environment (PettingZoo / OpenAI Gym): A custom simulated continuous 3D 
space where multiple agents interact, observe their surroundings, and receive localized rewards. 
• 
Reinforcement Learning Engine (Ray RLlib): A highly scalable distributed computing framework 
utilized to train the MAPPO algorithms across multiple CPU cores. 
• 
Deep Neural Networks (PyTorch): The underlying actor-critic networks determining the 
continuous flight paths of the drones based on LiDAR-like state observations. 
• 
3D Simulation UI (React & Three.js): A visualizer that ingests the live model inference 
coordinates and renders the swarm's flight paths and sensor coverage in 3D.
