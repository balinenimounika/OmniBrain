Common Features Across All Projects 
• 
Distributed Computing Architectures: Moving beyond running models in a single Jupyter 
Notebook. These projects utilize distributed frameworks like Ray, Flower, and LangGraph to 
coordinate workloads across multiple agents, nodes, or CPU cores. 
• 
Advanced Neural Architectures: Utilizing the absolute forefront of model architectures, 
including Vision-Language Models (VLMs), 3D U-Nets for medical imaging, and Multi-Agent 
Actor-Critic networks. 
• 
Complex Data Privacy & Guardrails: Enterprise AI requires strict safety measures. These 
projects implement NeMo Guardrails for LLMs, Homomorphic Encryption, and Differential 
Privacy to ensure mathematical security. 
• 
Interactive Visualization: Complex AI outputs are useless if they cannot be interpreted. These 
systems feature React and WebGL dashboards to render agent reasoning graphs, federated 
training metrics, and 3D drone simulations.
